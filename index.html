Research CV

Research

Common-envelope evolution

Hydrodynamics, orbital evolution, drag, jets, and the physics of interacting binary stars during common-envelope phases.

Galactic magnetism

Galactic dynamos, magnetic-field structure, synchrotron emission, Faraday rotation, and the evolution of magnetic fields in galaxies.

Astrophysical fluids & plasmas

Computational studies of fluid and plasma processes relevant to stars, binaries, galaxies, and the interstellar medium.
